# Orb Surge - Game Design Document

## 1. Concept & Hook (web-first, instant engagement)

**Core Concept**: A fast-paced physics-based puzzle game where players launch magnetic orbs to collect scattered energy particles while avoiding electromagnetic hazards.

**Instant Hook**: 
- One-click/tap to aim and launch orbs
- Satisfying magnetic attraction visual effects
- Immediate visual feedback with particle trails and screen shake
- Score multipliers create "one more try" addiction
- Clear visual goals: collect all glowing particles to advance

**Unique Selling Points**:
- Magnetic physics create unpredictable, skill-based gameplay
- Procedurally generated levels ensure infinite replayability
- Risk/reward mechanics: closer shots = higher multipliers but more danger
- Visual spectacle with particle effects and smooth animations

## 2. Core Loop (sessions 2-10 min, replayable)

**Primary Loop (30-60 seconds)**:
1. **Aim**: Mouse/touch to set trajectory angle and power
2. **Launch**: Release to fire magnetic orb
3. **Collect**: Orb attracts nearby energy particles automatically
4. **Avoid**: Navigate around electromagnetic hazards (repulsion fields, black holes)
5. **Score**: Earn points based on particles collected and proximity bonuses
6. **Progress**: Complete level or retry for better score

**Secondary Loop (2-5 minutes)**:
1. Complete 5-10 levels in a themed "Sector"
2. Unlock new orb types and abilities
3. Face sector boss (large collection challenge)
4. Advance to next sector with new hazards/mechanics

**Meta Loop (session-to-session)**:
- Persistent high scores and best times
- Unlock new orb skins and trail effects
- Achievement system for trick shots and combos

## 3. Mechanics (keyboard+mouse+touch controls)

**Core Mechanics**:
- **Magnetic Attraction**: Orbs automatically pull in energy particles within radius
- **Trajectory Physics**: Realistic arc-based projectile motion
- **Hazard Interaction**: Electromagnetic fields push/pull orbs off course
- **Combo System**: Collecting multiple particles rapidly increases multiplier
- **Limited Shots**: Each level has optimal shot count for 3-star rating

**Control Scheme**:
- **Mouse**: Move to aim, hold and drag for power, release to launch
- **Keyboard**: Arrow keys for fine aim adjustment, Spacebar to launch, R to restart
- **Touch**: Tap and drag from orb position, release to launch
- **Universal**: M to mute, P to pause (no Escape key usage)

**Advanced Mechanics** (unlocked progressively):
- **Orb Types**: Heavy (breaks through barriers), Light (affected more by fields), Splitting (creates multiple collection points)
- **Power-ups**: Magnetic boost, particle magnetism, trajectory prediction
- **Environmental Elements**: Moving platforms, rotating hazards, gravity wells

## 4. Levels & Progression (clear goals per session)

**Level Structure**:
- **Goal**: Collect all energy particles (typically 5-15 per level)
- **Par**: Target number of shots for optimal score
- **Star Rating**: 1-3 stars based on shots used and time taken
- **Progression**: Must collect 70% of particles to advance

**Difficulty Curve**:
- **Levels 1-10**: Basic aiming and collection
- **Levels 11-25**: Introduction of repulsion hazards
- **Levels 26-40**: Moving obstacles and multiple orb types
- **Levels 41-55**: Complex electromagnetic fields and precision shots
- **Levels 56+**: Procedurally generated infinite content

**Sector Themes**:
1. **Asteroid Field**: Basic physics, static obstacles
2. **Solar Storm**: Electromagnetic interference, particle streams
3. **Black Hole Region**: Gravity wells and trajectory distortion
4. **Energy Nexus**: Rapid-fire collection challenges
5. **Quantum Zone**: Phase-shifting obstacles and multi-dimensional collection
